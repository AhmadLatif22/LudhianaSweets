Our Story

A Taste Carried Across Generations

Ludhiana Sweets began with a single recipe brought over from Ludhiana and kept alive in a small kitchen in Sargodha. What started as barfi made for family and neighbours became a name people trust for celebrations, gifts, and quiet everyday indulgence.

We still cook the same way in small batches, over slow heat, because that's the only way this barfi has ever tasted right.
