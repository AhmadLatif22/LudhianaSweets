1. Who we are

This website, ludhianasweets.com (the “Website”), is operated by Ludhiana Sweets (“we”, “us”, “our”), a traditional sweet maker based at 548 D Block, Satellite Town, Sargodha, Punjab, Pakistan. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit the Website or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account to shop with us. We collect information in the following ways.

Information you give us

  • Order details: your full name, phone number, email address, delivery address, city, postal code, any delivery notes you add, and the items you order.
  • Reviews: the name, star rating and comment you submit about a product. Reviews are checked by us before they appear on the Website.
  • Messages: anything you send us by the contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp, together with your contact details.

Information created when you order

  • Your order number, order total, delivery charge, payment method and payment status, order status history, and courier and tracking details once your order is dispatched.

Technical information

  • Like most websites, our hosting and security systems automatically record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested. We use it to keep the Website running, secure and free from abuse.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as health, religion or financial account details. Please do not include such information in delivery notes or messages.

4. WhatsApp, email and calls

After you place an order we may contact you on the phone number you provided, by WhatsApp message or phone call, to confirm your order and delivery details. We may also email you a receipt and delivery updates if you gave an email address. These are service messages about your order, not marketing. WhatsApp messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business platform provided by Meta, which has its own privacy practices.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run the shop, and only the information they need to do their job:

  • Delivery partners and couriers receive your name, phone number and delivery address so they can deliver your order.
  • Website hosting and database providers store and process Website data on our behalf. We use Vercel for hosting and Supabase for our order database.
  • Email and messaging providers send your receipts and order messages. We use Resend for email and WhatsApp Business for WhatsApp messages.
  • Payment providers: at present we accept Cash on Delivery only, so no card or wallet details are collected on the Website. If we introduce online payments, they will be processed by a licensed payment provider, and we will not see or store your full card number.
  • Authorities and advisers where we are legally required to disclose information, or to protect our rights, customers or the public, for example to investigate fraud.

Some of these providers operate servers outside Pakistan, so your information may be processed in other countries. We choose providers that apply appropriate security safeguards.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this Website. To make shopping work, the Website saves your shopping cart in your browser’s local storage so that it is still there if you refresh the page or come back later. We also keep a private order-access token in your browser during checkout so you can view your order confirmation. This data stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings (clearing it will empty your cart).

7. How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as we need it to deliver your order, handle any refund or complaint, and meet our accounting, tax and legal obligations. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies you. Reviews you submit stay on the Website until you ask us to remove them.

8. How we protect it

Our Website uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Order information is held in access-controlled systems, and only authorised members of our team can view orders. We never store customer data in a way that is publicly readable. No system on the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and review them regularly.

9. Your choices and rights

You can contact us at any time to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your information, or to remove a review you wrote;
  • object to any message you do not want to receive from us.

We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep certain records where the law or our accounting obligations require it (for example, a record of a completed sale). We aim to reply within 30 days. To make a request, email us using the details in the “Contact us” section below.

10. Children

Our Website is intended for adults placing orders for themselves or their families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a new service such as online payments. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make a significant change, we will highlight it on the Website.
